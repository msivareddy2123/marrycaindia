Questions people ask first

Who can join?+

CAs, CMAs, CSs and anyone from any other profession. For CA, CMA and CS, any stage works: Foundation, Intermediate, Final or qualified. Parents and guardians can register on behalf of their son or daughter.

How does verification work?+

You enter your ICAI, ICMAI or ICSI membership or registration number. We check it against the institute record and show a verified badge once it matches. Members from other professions are labelled as not institute-verified.

What does it cost?+

The first 30 days are free. After that, you can pay ₹1,499 for 3 months or ₹4,999 for 1 year.

Who sees my photo and phone number?+

Only people you both agree to connect with. Until then, others see your profile details without your photo or contact information.
