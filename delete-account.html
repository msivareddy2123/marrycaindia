Delete Your Account

Last updated: 25 November 2026

You can delete your MarryCAindia account and your data at any time. There are two ways.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Log in at marrycaindia.com/app.html or open the MarryCAindia app.
  2. Go to Me and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you cannot log in, email support@marrycaindia.com from the email address you registered with. Write "Delete my account" and your registered name. We will delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photos, interests, shortlist and chats
  • Your login (email and phone)
  • Your verification request and membership number

What may be kept

We may keep limited records for up to 90 days where the law requires it, for example payment records and abuse or fraud reports. After that they are removed.
