Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

MarryCAindia.com ("MarryCAindia", "we", "us") is a matrimony service for CA, CMA, CS and every profession in India. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Registration details: who is registering (bride, groom, parent or guardian), full name, age, profession, stage, city, WhatsApp number, email address and a few lines about you.
  • Login details: your email, a password (stored only in scrambled form, never in plain text) and a login token.
  • Profile details you add later in the member area, such as family details, partner preferences, photos and professional information.
  • Technical data your browser stores on your device, such as your language choice and login token.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and manage your profile and show it to suitable members.
  • To verify your email with a one-time code and keep your account secure.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp or email about your profile, matches and service updates.
  • To improve the site and prevent fake profiles and misuse.

3. Who can see your information

Other members see your profile details such as profession, stage, city and your story. Photos and contact details (phone, WhatsApp, email) are shared only after both sides agree to connect. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Services we use

To run the site we use trusted providers: GitHub Pages for hosting, and Google Sheets and Google Apps Script to store sign-up details and send email codes. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. You can ask us to pause or delete your profile at any time, and we will delete or anonymise your data unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information.
  • Withdraw your consent to be contacted.
  • Pause or delete your profile from the member area or by writing to us.

7. Security

We use reasonable steps to protect your data, including email verification and scrambled passwords. No online service is completely secure, so please keep your password private and do not share your one-time codes.

8. Children

This service is for adults who are of legal marriage age. Parents and guardians may register on behalf of a son or daughter who is of legal marriage age.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact us

Questions or requests: support@marrycaindia.com

This policy is a general statement for a new service and is not legal advice. Please have it reviewed by a lawyer before large-scale launch.
