Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Cancellation

You can stop using MarryCAindia at any time. Premium plans do not renew automatically. Your access continues until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds

  • The first 30 days are free, so you can try the service before paying.
  • After a plan is activated, the fee is not refundable, because the service is delivered right away.
  • If you are charged twice for the same plan, or you pay and the plan is not activated, we will refund the extra or failed amount in full.
  • If we close your account for a policy violation, no refund is given.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

How to ask

Email support@marrycaindia.com with your registered email and the payment date and amount. We reply within 2 working days.
