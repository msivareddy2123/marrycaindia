About Us

Last updated: 25 November 2026

MarryCAindia is a matrimony service in India for Chartered Accountants (CA), Cost Accountants (CMA), Company Secretaries (CS) and members of every other profession. Students, qualified professionals, doctors, engineers, teachers and business owners are all welcome.

What we do

We help families find a suitable match. Members create a profile, search with filters such as profession, language, community and location, send interests and chat after both sides accept. Members can ask us to verify their ICAI, ICMAI or ICSI membership.

Who runs it

MarryCAindia is run as a sole proprietorship in India. We are not affiliated with ICAI, ICMAI or ICSI.
